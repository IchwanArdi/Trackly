# Trackly Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Trackly Data Governance

Trackly is committed to providing a calm, private activity-tracking environment. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we store, how it is secured, and your rights over your personal history.

## 01. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum necessary information to provide personal habit tracking:

- Account credentials: Name and email address (via registration or Google Sign-In).
- Activity history: Categories, entries, numeric values, completion logs, and personal notes.

## 02. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to calculate statistics, streaks, and heatmap charts for your personal workspace. We never sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties or advertising networks.

## 03. Google Sign-In Scope

When logging in via Google OAuth, we strictly read your basic profile (name and email) to authorize your account. We request zero additional Google scopes or access permissions.

## 04. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL database instances hosted via Supabase, served securely through Vercel over TLS. Passwords are salted and hashed using industry-standard cryptography.

## 05. Data Retention & Control

Your records remain active for as long as your account exists. You maintain full rights to export your data or delete your account permanently at any time.

## 06. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full rights to request complete deletion of your account and logs. Visit our [Data Deletion Instructions](https://tracklyid.vercel.app/data-deletion) for step-by-step guidance.
