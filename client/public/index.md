# Trackly — Build calm routines. Observe steady progress.

Trackly is a free, privacy-first habit and activity tracker for recording daily routines, observing momentum, and staying consistent without gamified noise.

- Live app: https://tracklyid.vercel.app
- Author: Ichwan Ardi — https://github.com/IchwanArdi
- Contact: ichwanpwt22@gmail.com

## What you can do

- Record daily habits and activities in seconds.
- Create custom categories with names, units, colors, and icons.
- Track numeric values such as minutes, pages, cups, or kilometers — or use a simple checkmark.
- Build consecutive-day streaks.
- View a 90-day contribution heatmap of your consistency.
- Share progress summaries as image cards.
- Use the same account across phone and desktop.
- Delete individual entries or your entire account at any time.

## How it works

1. Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or continue with Google.
2. Add the routines you want to track, each with its own unit and color.
3. Log an entry whenever you complete an activity.
4. Review streaks, heatmaps, and progress summaries in the dashboard.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Trackly free to use?

Yes. Trackly is completely free for personal activity and habit tracking — no premium tiers, no ads.

### How do I install Trackly on my mobile phone?

Click the "Get App" or "Download App" button in the navigation header. On iOS or Android, select "Add to Home Screen" to install Trackly as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

### Can I track habits with custom units like minutes or liters?

Yes. Every category can be customized with specific target units (e.g. Minutes, Pages, Cups, Km) or left as a simple checkmark.

### Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or continue instantly with your Google account. New accounts are created after the email code is verified.

### Can I use Trackly on multiple devices?

Yes. Your data is tied to your account, so you can log activities on your phone and review your progress on your laptop — everything stays in sync.

### Is my activity data private?

Your entries are only accessible when signed in to your account. Your personal data is never sold or shared.

### Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete individual entries anytime, or permanently remove your entire account and all associated data from your profile — no questions asked.

### Does Trackly work offline?

After installing, the app shell is cached so Trackly opens instantly, even on a weak connection. Logging and syncing need an internet connection so your data stays safely stored with your account.

## Technology

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Router, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL (Supabase), and Vercel.
